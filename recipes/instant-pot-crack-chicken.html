Neighbor Raymond's pick — he says his kids love it. A shredded chicken loaded with ranch seasoning, cream cheese, cheddar, and bacon, built almost entirely hands-off in the pressure cooker. Everything but the finishing touches goes in raw; 12 minutes at pressure does the heavy lifting. Serve piled on buns, over rice, or tossed with pasta.
