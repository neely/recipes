Caldeirada

Prep15 min
Cook30 min
Total45 min
Serves4
SkillEasy
Scale

A garlicky, wine-and-tomato fish (or chicken) stew, finished with a single layer of thinly sliced potato that steams on top instead of getting stirred in. Loose on exact vegetable amounts by design — vary the peppers, swap proteins, use sweet potato instead of yukon gold. Scales easily for a crowd: multiply everything but the potato, since a thin layer is enough regardless of batch size, and give it a little extra time to come up to a boil.
